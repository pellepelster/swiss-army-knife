# Android

There are two ways to use SAK Notes, SAK Grocery List and SAK Todo on an Android phone:

- **[The web apps](web-apps.md):** installed from Chrome or Firefox in a few taps.
- **[The native apps](native-apps.md):** downloaded with a link of your SAK instance.

## Apps from outside of Google Play

The native apps do not come from Google Play, and Android makes such apps hard to install:

- The browser, Android and Play Protect each show a warning, and some make the app look dangerous.
- You must allow the browser to install apps, and later the app itself, for its updates.
- The words differ on each phone and Android version, so the steps can look different on yours.

The web apps have none of this: no download, no warnings, and the browser keeps them up to date. If you do not need
the native apps, [install the web apps](web-apps.md).
